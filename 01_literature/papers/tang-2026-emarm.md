## Paper findings (within the scope above)

EmArm integrates large-area flexible tactile skin, proprioception, and closed-loop perception/action, demonstrating touch-intent recognition and tactile-driven replanning. Source: [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44460-026-00097-1)

## Potential use in our project (design inference)

Compare the information available from whole-arm tactile sensing, gripper tactile sensing, and wrist F/T rather than treating them as interchangeable.

**First question to test:** Do we need information about the gripper's grasp, or about human touches on the robot arm?

## What this does not establish

The system uses specialized whole-arm skin hardware. It is not direct validation of a single tactile gripper or cooperative workpiece flipping.
